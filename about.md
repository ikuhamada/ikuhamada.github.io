## Biography

I am a computational materials physicist specializing in the development and
application of electronic structure methods based on density functional theory
and many-body perturbation theory.
My research focuses on surfaces and interfaces relevant to energy,
electrochemistry, and environmental materials.

## Academic Positions
- Professor, Department of Precision Engineering, The University of Osaka
- Associate Professor, The University of Osaka
- Senior Researcher, National Institute for Materials Science (NIMS)
- Assistant Professor, WPI-Advanced Institute for Materials Research, Tohoku University

## Education
- Ph.D. in Science, Osaka University (2003)
- M.Sc. in Science, Osaka University (1999)
- B.Sc. in Science, Osaka University (1997)
