# Ikutaro Hamada

Professor, Department of Precision Engineering
Graduate School of Engineering, The University of Osaka

I am a computational materials physicist working on the development and application
of first-principles electronic structure methods for surface and interface science.

## Research Interests
- Density functional theory
- Density functional perturbation theory
- Many-body perturbation theory
- Surface and interface science
- Water, ice, and molecular crystals
