## Overview

My research aims to understand the electronic, vibrational, and chemical properties of surfaces and interfaces at the atomic and sub-molecular scales using first-principles simulations.

## Method Development
- Density functional theory (DFT)
- Density functional perturbation theory (DFPT)
- Many-body perturbation theory (GW/BSE)
- van der Waals density functionals
- Electron-phonon coupling

## Applications
- Surface and interface electronic structures
- Water-surface and ice-surface interactions
- Electrochemical interfaces
- Molecular crystals and organic-metal interfaces
